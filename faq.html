VoltWorth FAQ

Questions about plans, access, and product availability.

This FAQ reflects the proposed commercial model in the offline prototype. Pricing and trial terms remain proposals until final launch economics and policies are approved.

What Scanner plans are being proposed?

VoltWorth Scanner is proposed at $24.99 per month or $249 per year. VoltWorth Scanner Pro is proposed at $44.99 per month or $449 per year. These are not active billing terms yet.

What is the difference between Scanner and Scanner Pro?

Both plans retain the core Ticker Scanner, KLine charting, Technical Grade, Outlook, projections, Trade Setup, research context, holdings, and web access. Pro adds Big Upside Scanner, expanded performance/history features, substantially higher AI usage, priority support, and selected early-access features.

What trial is being proposed?

A 7-day no-credit-card web trial is proposed. The trial would expose Scanner Pro web features, include up to 5 AI-enhanced analyses, and would not provide the Windows download until a paid subscription is active.

Will annual plans cost less than paying monthly?

Yes under the current proposal. The $249 Scanner annual price and $449 Scanner Pro annual price are each approximately 17% less than paying the corresponding monthly price for twelve months.

Will VoltWorth Scanner work in a web browser?

That remains the primary planned commercial delivery model. Subscribers would authenticate, have their entitlement checked, and launch the hosted Scanner dashboard.

Will both paid Scanner plans include the Windows edition?

That is the current proposal. Once the Windows subscriber edition is commercially packaged, both paid Scanner plans would be eligible to use it. Trial users would not receive the downloadable edition.

Why is AI usage limited?

AI enhancement has a variable operating cost and should remain a controlled entitlement. The proposed limits are 10 enhanced analyses per month for Scanner and 100 per month for Scanner Pro rather than advertising unlimited usage.

Does the Scanner execute trades?

No. VoltWorth Scanner is positioned as a market-intelligence and research product and does not execute trades from the dashboard.

Are VoltWorth Alpha or VoltWorth Options Alpha included in Scanner Pro?

No. They remain separate coming-soon products. Their pricing and customer availability will be decided only after their automated trading systems complete the required validation and commercial-readiness reviews.

What is VoltWorth Complete?

VoltWorth Complete remains the future bundle concept for users who eventually want Scanner plus VoltWorth Alpha and VoltWorth Options Alpha after those products become commercially available.

Are these prices final?

No. Before publishing final prices, VoltWorth needs to validate commercial market-data, news, API, hosting, AI, support, and other per-subscriber costs.

What happens before real subscriptions go live?

The Pricing, Terms, Privacy, billing flow, authentication architecture, product entitlements, data licensing, and security controls must all be finalized before real customer subscriptions or personal-data collection begins.
