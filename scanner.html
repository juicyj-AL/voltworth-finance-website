Core experience

Research without losing the thread.

VoltWorth Scanner combines charting, technical analysis, projections, event context, portfolio views, and trade-setup tracking in one dashboard.

  • Ticker Scanner with KLine charting, multiple chart modes, drawing tools, and indicators.
  • Technical Matrix, Technical Grade, Ticker Outlook, projections, catalysts, earnings, insiders, and news context.
  • Big Upside Scanner for ranked opportunity discovery across selected market universes.
  • Personal Holdings Scanner with performance, recommendation context, and portfolio workflow tools.
  • Trade Setup history and outcome tracking designed for measurable follow-through.
  • Optional AI-enhanced analysis layered on top of deterministic local analytics.
